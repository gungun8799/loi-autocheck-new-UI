Validate deposit requirements for LO (Lease Offer) contracts.

Extract: Monthly rental rate, Monthly service charge, Total Rent Deposit, Customer Name, Contract Number, Tenant Selection, Building ID
Note: Total Monthly Amount = Monthly rental rate + Monthly service charge

--- LO CONTRACT DEPOSIT VALIDATION ---
• LO contracts are new lease offers requiring stricter deposit validation
• Multiple deposit rules may apply based on tenant and building status

Validation Rules:

"Total Rent Deposit":
• **Priority Rule**: Big Tenant Override
  – If Customer in bigtenant.xlsx → deposit must be ≥ 3 × (Monthly rental rate + Monthly service charge)
  – If deposit < (rate + service) × 3 → invalid, reason: "Customer in bigtenant list; deposit must be at least 3 × total monthly charges"

• **LO Special Rule**: Tenant Selection Check
  – If Contract Number contains "LO" AND "Tenant Selection" = "No" → deposit must be ≥ 4 × (Monthly rental rate + Monthly service charge)
  – If deposit < (rate + service) × 4 → invalid, reason: "LO contract with Tenant Selection=No requires 4 × total monthly charges deposit"

• **Building Rule**: Master_9_cell Check
  – If Building ID exists in Master_9_cell.xlsx → deposit may be ≥ 2 × (Monthly rental rate + Monthly service charge)
  – If deposit < (rate + service) × 2 → invalid, reason: "Building requires minimum 2 × total monthly charges deposit"

• **Default Rule**: Standard Deposit
  – Deposit must be ≥ 3 × (Monthly rental rate + Monthly service charge)
  – If deposit < (rate + service) × 3 → invalid, reason: "Standard deposit requirement: 3 × total monthly charges"

Return validation result in JSON format:
```json
[
  {
    "field": "Total Rent Deposit",
    "value": "45000",
    "valid": true,
    "reason": "LO contract meets 4 × monthly rate requirement (15000 × 4 = 60000)"
  }
]
```
