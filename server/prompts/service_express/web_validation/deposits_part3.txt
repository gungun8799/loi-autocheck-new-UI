Validate Tenancy Deposit from WEB - PART 3 (Default Rule). Return JSON array.

Extract from Simplicity data: Monthly Amount of rent, Monthly Service Charge, Total Deposits
Note: Total Monthly Amount = Monthly Amount of rent + Monthly Service Charge

**DEFAULT RULE (if Parts 1 & 2 didn't apply):**

Total Deposits MUST be at least 3.00 × (Monthly Amount of rent + Monthly Service Charge)

If Total Deposits < 3.00 × (Monthly Amount of rent + Monthly Service Charge):
  Return: {"field":"Tenancy Deposit","value":"[amount]","valid":false,"reason":"Default Rule = 3 months of total charges required (Rent+Service)"}

If Total Deposits ≥ 3.00 × (Monthly Amount of rent + Monthly Service Charge):
  Return: {"field":"Tenancy Deposit","value":"[amount]","valid":true,"reason":"Default Rule = 3 months of total charges met (Rent+Service)"}

Example calculations:
- Rent: 42,436.00, Service: 0, Total Required: 127,308.00
- Rent: 30,000.00, Service: 10,000.00, Total Required: 120,000.00

Note: Handle web formatting (commas, currency symbols)
