Extract from Simplicity data: Contract Number, Property Type, Brand Name, Monthly Amount of rent, Monthly Service Charge, Total Deposits
Note: Total Monthly Amount = Monthly Amount of rent + Monthly Service Charge

**CHECK THESE RULES FIRST:**

1. **Service Express Low Rent Exception**: For Service Express contracts, if Monthly Amount of rent ≤ 5000
   - If Total Deposits < 1 × (Monthly Amount of rent + Monthly Service Charge): ❌ Invalid "Service Express: Low rent (≤5000) - Total Deposits (<deposit>) is less than required 1 × total monthly charges (=<total>)"
   - Else: ✅ Valid "Service Express: Low rent (≤5000) - Total Deposits (<deposit>) is acceptable (≥ 1 × total monthly charges)"

2. **ATM SCB Exception**: If Brand Name contains "ATM SCB" (case-insensitive)
   - If Total Deposits == 0: ✅ Valid "Brand Name contains ATM SCB; Total Deposits may be zero"
   - Else: ✅ Valid "Brand Name contains ATM SCB; Total Deposits (<deposit>) is acceptable (≥ 0)"

3. **ATM KBANK & Go Fresh Exception**: If Brand Name contains "ATM KBANK" AND Property Type is "Go Fresh"
   - If Total Deposits < 1 × (Monthly Amount of rent + Monthly Service Charge): ❌ Invalid "Brand Name contains ATM KBANK and Property Type is Go Fresh; Total Deposits (<deposit>) is less than 1 × total monthly charges (=<total>)"
   - Else: ✅ Valid "Brand Name contains ATM KBANK and Property Type is Go Fresh; Total Deposits (<deposit>) ≥ 1 × total monthly charges (=<total>)"

**If none of the above rules match, return:**
[{"field":"Tenancy Deposit","value":"[amount]","valid":null,"reason":"No Part 1 rules matched - proceed to Part 2"}]
