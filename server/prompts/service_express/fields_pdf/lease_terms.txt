**IMPORTANT:** For Service Express contracts, focus on rental amounts and basic lease information.

Required fields:
"Rental Rate Before Tax (Monthly)" - **PDF PATTERNS:** Look for "ค่าเช่าต่อเดือน" or "ค่าเช่า" followed by amount in Thai Baht. Extract numeric value without "บาท" or currency symbols.

"Lease term (year)" - **PDF PATTERNS:** Look for lease duration in Thai text. Common patterns: "X ปี" (X years), "จำนวน X ปี" (number of X years), or duration text near contract period descriptions.

"Lease term (month)" - **PDF PATTERNS:** Look for additional months beyond full years. Patterns: "X เดือน" (X months), or calculate remaining months from total lease period.

"Minimum Advance Notice" - **PDF PATTERNS:** Look for advance notice requirements in Thai contract text. Common patterns include notice periods for termination or renewal.

"Contract Start Date" - **PDF PATTERNS:** Look for "วันที่เริ่มสัญญา" or "เริ่มวันที่" followed by Thai date format. Convert to standard YYYY-MM-DD format.

"Contract End Date" - **PDF PATTERNS:** Look for "วันที่สิ้นสุดสัญญา" or "สิ้นสุดวันที่" followed by Thai date format. Convert to standard YYYY-MM-DD format.
